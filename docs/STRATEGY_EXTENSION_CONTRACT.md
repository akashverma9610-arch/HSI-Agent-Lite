# Modular strategy extension contract

The versioned registry is a **knowledge index**, not a strategy engine. The resolution rule is fail-closed: an unknown ID/version, a proposed or review-only family, missing approval, or missing separate activation resolves to `STRATEGY_UNAVAILABLE` for active decision use. Registry presence does not imply approval; approval does not imply runtime activation. A selected research candidate is not an active policy.

Every new family or version must provide a new immutable catalog file and registry entry declaring: (1) stable strategy ID and family, (2) version, (3) purpose, (4) intended and validated horizon status, (5) required normalized evidence and source/PIT contracts, (6) mandatory/optional/context/quarantined Dxx diagnostics, (7) deterministic eligibility references and insufficiency behavior, (8) interpretive guidance, (9) all six V1 gate applicability decisions with reasons, (10) liquidity and exitability policy, (11) entry policy, (12) invalidation policy, (13) target policy, (14) portfolio/Watch/ranking implications and explicit action permissions, (15) blockers, (16) pinned provenance, (17) authority/lifecycle/activation status, and (18) validation evidence. An unavailable item is recorded as `UNAVAILABLE` or null, never filled by analogy to another strategy. Source family IDs, diagnostic IDs and gate IDs must resolve to their existing catalogs. A strategy consumes, but never recomputes, technical/fundamental metrics owned by evidence families or tools.

Version path: immutable Strategy v1 → research proposal → separately identified candidate v2 → chronological backtest, out-of-sample and prospective/PIT evidence → human review → approved version → **separate prospective activation**. Do not overwrite v1, relabel historical decisions, or promote a draft by file recency. AI may propose hypotheses or improvements, but cannot approve, select or activate a version. Any change to geometry, horizon, liquidity, score use, gate applicability or portfolio permissions requires its own policy/version review. No universal entry, stop, target, score minimum or holding horizon is inherited.

Until a separately approved runtime exists, no registry item computes eligibility or emits an order. A strategy match is not gate PASS; any applicable gate FAIL remains blocking regardless of score or AI interpretation. Existing normalized evidence and the 61 diagnostic catalog can be reused without architectural redesign. New evidence definitions are added upstream through their own authority and source-readiness review, not calculated inside the strategy.
