# Strategy Confluence — non-runtime contract

Confluence is a descriptive synthesis of zero, one, or several **separately eligible** strategy-archetype matches for the same canonical instrument and compatible point-in-time cutoff. It is not the number of strategies over a score threshold, a new global score, a gate result, an order, or portfolio authority. Task #008 adds no matcher, numeric archetype score, confluence calculator, activation threshold, broker action or runtime. Every taxonomy archetype remains design-only and unavailable for active decisions.

The existing Core V1 0–10 Decision Score is broad analytical compression. A future archetype-specific score, if separately approved, would describe fit for that opportunity type. Confluence would describe the diversity and overlap of *eligible* fits. These are three distinct objects: one stock may eventually fit Turnaround strongly and Compounder weakly without averaging the two into a universal strategy number. Global score completeness, strategy eligibility, confluence support and six-gate readiness are independent states.

`STRATEGY_CONFLUENCE_MODEL.json` declares possible outputs: `NO_CONFLUENCE`, `SINGLE_STRATEGY`, `MULTI_STRATEGY_SAME_FAMILY`, `CROSS_FAMILY_CONFLUENCE`, `HIGH_DIVERSITY_CONFLUENCE`, and `UNRESOLVED`. `NO_CONFLUENCE` requires complete applicable evaluation with no match; missing evidence is `UNRESOLVED`, never zero. `HIGH_DIVERSITY_CONFLUENCE` has no current rule or numeric cutoff and cannot be emitted by this knowledge package. Exact numeric thresholds, weights, probabilities and activation rules remain future calibration and human-review work.

`STRATEGY_OVERLAP_MATRIX.json` specifies ordered, testable *future* relationship rules, not 35×35 hand-entered conclusions. Same archetype, exact event or selected policy, or identical dependency lineage is `HIGHLY_OVERLAPPING`; same primary family is `SAME_FAMILY_CORRELATED`; cross-family shared event/evidence/diagnostic/source lineage is `PARTIALLY_OVERLAPPING`; complete disjoint lineages across primary families may be `INDEPENDENT`; missing or conflicting overlap proof is `UNRESOLVED`. Secondary-family tags alone prove no independence. Five correlated price/flow matches cannot count as five independent confirmations. A shared event cannot be duplicated into independent catalyst votes. Even an `INDEPENDENT` classification is descriptive, not a numeric confluence award or capital decision.

For overlap, “source lineage” means the **underlying market, financial or event evidence**. The common Task #008 design brief or shared taxonomy artifact provenance is not market evidence and must not itself make every pair overlapping.

Future comparison must retain exact archetype and policy versions, normalized evidence IDs, diagnostic IDs, event identity, source fingerprints, as-of/known-at and availability, missingness and conflict lineage. Unknown strategy/version remains `STRATEGY_UNAVAILABLE` under the Task #007 registry. If overlap or coverage cannot be verified, report `UNRESOLVED`; never infer independence from absence of recorded overlap. No LLM opinion, global score, strategy score or confluence label may override a failed applicable V1 gate. Gate PASS alone does not make a good stock or authorize execution.
