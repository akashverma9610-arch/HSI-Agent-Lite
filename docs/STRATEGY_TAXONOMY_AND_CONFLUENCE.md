## Dependency philosophy

The ten canonical dimensions are `FUNDAMENTALS`, `EARNINGS`, `TECHNICALS`, `VALUATION`, `CATALYST_NEWS`, `SECTOR_MACRO`, `FLOW`, `BALANCE_SHEET_CASH_FLOW`, `MANAGEMENT_GOVERNANCE`, and `LIQUIDITY_EXITABILITY`. Every archetype marks each `PRIMARY`, `SECONDARY`, `OPTIONAL` or `NOT_REQUIRED`. The 350-row `STRATEGY_DEPENDENCY_MATRIX.json` maps all 35 × 10 intersections to Task #005 evidence-family and Dxx IDs or explicit unavailable future proof. The matrix is a **design dependency**, not an instruction to run all referenced diagnostics now. An archetype should retrieve only relevant, PIT-valid evidence; a `NOT_REQUIRED` dimension is not fetched by default. Founder designs may identify future required inputs, but cannot turn a currently missing source or quarantined D35/D57 into a valid result.

The declarative stages deliberately differ. Fundamental Inflection starts with business change and earnings propagation; technicals are confirmation. Pre-Breakout centers sector/base/volatility/volume/flow/timing, while its narrower legacy selected-policy path remains Watch-only and cannot automatically bless founder accumulation language. Mid-Term Compounder centers runway, quality, growth, ROCE/FCF, management and valuation. Turnaround starts with a documented problem and requires operational, cash and debt recovery before price confirmation. These are thought structures, **not executable algorithms or thresholds**. The same stock may eventually fit several archetypes differently; averaging every strategy into one number would erase those differences.

`TASK007_TO_TAXONOMY_MAP.json` maps all six prior entries. Pre-Breakout maps partially to Pre-Breakout Accumulation; Tactical and Positional are umbrellas, not extra archetypes; Long-Term decomposes into distinct research archetypes; Event/Catalyst and Market-Red have exact conceptual-name mappings but remain proposed/non-active. No Task #007 authority transfers automatically to a broader new archetype. The Task #007 unknown-strategy rule remains `STRATEGY_UNAVAILABLE`.

Global HSI Decision Score remains the existing ten-component 0–10 broad compression. Future archetype-specific eligibility or score would be a separate, versioned opportunity-fit policy; neither exists here. Confluence would summarize multiple supported fits while discounting evidence/event/family overlap. The overlap rules and unresolved behavior are in `STRATEGY_OVERLAP_MATRIX.json` and `STRATEGY_CONFLUENCE_MODEL.json`; no count-above-X formula or numerical confluence cutoff is approved. A strategy match is not gate PASS, does not override gate FAIL, and does not bypass portfolio heat or broker controls.

Task #003's strict provenance/status schemas and Task #004's evidence-discipline, historical-PIT, validation and strategy-registry policies remain upstream constraints. The founder taxonomy labels are `INTERPRETATION/INFERENCE`, never source `FACT`; missing or unverified evidence cannot become a neutral score, PASS, or independent confirmation. A registry row is identity/availability metadata, not product-policy approval.

Future whole-market design only: NSE universe → cheap deterministic filters → candidate set → family/archetype pre-screen → relevant evidence collection → separately approved strategy-specific evaluation → confluence → deep AI research for finalists → global context and applicable gates → governed synthesis. No scanning, provider, agent or UI runtime is added. To extend the taxonomy, add one immutable archetype artifact, dependency rows, relationship/overlap review, eligibility-policy proposal, validation/version record and only genuinely new upstream evidence families. Do not rewrite technical/fundamental engines, the 61 diagnostics, global score, six gates or any Master Agent architecture. The expanded `STRATEGY_EXTENSION_CONTRACT.md` governs separate approval and activation.
