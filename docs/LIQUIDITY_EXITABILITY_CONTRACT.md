# Market liquidity structure versus portfolio exitability

These are different questions. Rules D08/D51 locate repeated-price pools and
likely stop clusters; D52 describes sweep/break/reclaim patterns. They are
price/volume *proxies*. Exact broker stops, quantities and order-book
absorption are not observed by those rules.

Portfolio exitability asks whether a **specific position size** could
plausibly be unwound under current conditions. Future analysis would need
current position value/quantity, average traded volume and value, free float
where evidenced, expected exit days, market stress/circuits, fees and
slippage. A price-level liquidity pool cannot substitute for those facts.
Missing size, turnover, float or stress information makes exitability
unknown, not safe. Inability to exit safely may block a rotation or exit
even when another analytical state favors review.

No universal participation, exit-days, free-float or slippage threshold is
approved by this extraction. Gate 1 friction/tax, Gate 3 surveillance and
Gate 5 heat still apply independently. This contract grants no exit/order
authority.
